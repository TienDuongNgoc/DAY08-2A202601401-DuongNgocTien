How to make an enquiry via the Student Connect Online Portal

Step 1: Go to the Student Connect Online Portal, and click “Login as RMIT Student or Staff”

Step 2: Enter your Student number and Password in the login
screen

Step 3: After login, you can Click on the related Service type and start making an enquiry.
