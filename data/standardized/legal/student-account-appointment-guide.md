How to book an appointment using your student account

RMIT Classification: Trusted

This guide covers how a student can book an appointment on the Student Connect Portal

Step 1:

Log in to https://rmit.service-now.com/connect_vn with your student account and choose the

Services icon

Step 2:

Under Services, click on Book an appointment to open the booking form

RMIT Classification: Trusted

Step 3: Search for appointment slot using

search fields. Detailed explanation of each filed

is listed at the end of this guideline

Step 4: View available appointments presented – change search if none are available on

requested date range

Step 5: Select appointment

Step 6: Click Submit

Note: Once you have done all the steps above, you will receive a confirmation email. If you wish to

change a booking, you need to go back to the booking in My Requests and cancel the original and

make a new

booking.
